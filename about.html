About

The story behind the tacos, the beaches, and everything in between.

Why BeachAndTaco.com?

Because two of the greatest things on earth — incredible beaches and perfect tacos — deserve to be celebrated together. BeachAndTaco.com is a passion project born out of years of chasing waves, exploring coastal towns, and eating truly great tacos from San Diego to the Yucatán, from Bali to Portugal.

The idea is simple: we find the spots worth going out of your way for, and we put them on a map so you don't have to hunt. No sponsored posts, no algorithms — just honest picks from people who live for this stuff.

What You'll Find Here

Two things, done well:

Taco Spots — street carts, beachside shacks, hidden gems, and the occasional legendary sit-down joint. We love anything from Baja-style fish tacos to slow-braised birria, as long as it's the real deal.

Beaches — the stunning, the wild, the secret, and the iconic. Surfing beaches, snorkeling beaches, sunbathing beaches, and beaches where you can do all of the above while eating a taco.

The map on the home page pulls directly from our curated lists — updated regularly as we discover new favorites.
